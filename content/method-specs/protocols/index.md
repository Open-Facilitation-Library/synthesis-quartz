---
title: Protocols
---

A **protocol** is the runnable half of a [[method-specs/index|method spec]]: a facilitation method written as a portable, forkable spec — its stages, roles, facilitator prompts, and what carries from one stage to the next. Fork it, adapt it to your context, and run it on any capable runtime.

The format is being shaped now, with [weval](https://weval.org)-style evals as the companion half. Protocols will land here as the spec stabilises.

title: Protocols
---

A **protocol** is the runnable half of a [[method-specs/index|method spec]]: a facilitation method written as a portable, forkable spec — its stages, roles, facilitator prompts, and what carries from one stage to the next. Fork it, adapt it to your context, and run it on any capable runtime. **Harmonica** is the reference runtime.

Each protocol below lives as a full spec in the [method-specs registry](https://github.com/Open-Facilitation-Library/method-specs), paired with [weval](https://weval.org)-style evals — one per stage — that say whether it ran well. The format is settling at v0.2. Specs stay `draft` until an experienced practitioner of the method has reviewed the prompt wording.

## Protocols

- [[method-specs/protocols/dynamic-facilitation|Dynamic Facilitation]] — choice-creating on a hard, open question: every contribution drawn out and charted to four shared boards, with the group reaching breakthroughs by emergence rather than by forced agreement. (CC0; after Jim Rough.)
- [[method-specs/protocols/mosaiclab-deliberation|MosaicLab Deliberation]] — a citizens'-deliberation chain that takes a small mixed group from a clear remit, through balanced information, to recommendations refined to an 80% comfort threshold. (CC BY-NC 4.0; adapted from MosaicLab's _Facilitating Deliberation_.)
